Common questions

AI regulation, answered

What AI laws apply to my company right now?

It depends on where you operate and what your AI does. Most US deployers are touched by at least one state law plus the federal model-risk and NIST expectations, and anyone serving EU users falls under the EU AI Act. This tracker groups the rules by jurisdiction so you can find yours, and a short assessment call will map exactly which ones reach you.

Is SR 11-7 still the rule for AI model risk in banks?

No. SR 11-7 was superseded in April 2026. The replacement guidance is narrower, applies only to the largest banks, and expressly excludes generative and agentic AI, which leaves a real governance gap. Our full breakdown is linked in the US Federal section above.

What is ISO/IEC 42001 and do I need it?

ISO/IEC 42001 is the international standard for an AI management system. It is becoming the reference point buyers and regulators use to judge whether your AI is governed. Whether you need certification depends on your customers and risk exposure; our plain-English guide, linked above, walks through it.
